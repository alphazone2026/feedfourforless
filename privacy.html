Privacy & disclosures

What we collect

When you sign up we collect your first name and email address. We use them only to send you the meal plan you asked for and our emails about cheap family dinners and our own meal plans.

Who handles it

Our email list is run on Brevo, which stores your details and sends our emails. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Click it and you're off the list straight away. To have your details deleted completely, email us at feedfourforless@gmail.com.

This website

This site doesn't use its own cookies or tracking. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Affiliate links

Some links we share are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend things we think are genuinely useful for feeding a family on a budget.

Prices and recipes

Prices are Walmart US online prices on the date shown and change by store and region. Always cook meat to a safe internal temperature and check labels for allergens.
